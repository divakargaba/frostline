# Hydrate Alert Response Checklist

When an early warning system issues a hydrate ALERT, follow this systematic response:

## Immediate assessment (0-5 minutes)

1. **Confirm the sensor readings:** Verify that the pressure and temperature declines are real (not a sensor fault). Cross-check P-PDG vs P-TPT and T-PDG vs T-TPT.
2. **Check the subcooling margin:** If the margin is negative or near zero, the system is in or near the hydrate zone.
3. **Review the classifier output:** A high hydrate probability (>60%) with supporting physics data strengthens the case for action.
4. **Rule out look-alikes:** Is temperature dropping (hydrate) or stable (scaling/restriction)? Is the pattern monotonic (hydrate) or oscillatory (slugging)?

## Intervention decision (5-15 minutes)

5. **If ALERT confirmed:** Initiate inhibitor injection through the chemical injection line. Standard methanol dose: use Hammerschmidt equation for required temperature depression plus 3 C safety margin.
6. **If uncertain (WATCH):** Increase monitoring frequency. Recheck in 15 minutes. Do not inject yet — unnecessary inhibitor injection wastes chemicals and may mask the true diagnosis.
7. **If dismissed:** Log the event for analysis. Monitor for recurrence.

## Post-intervention (15-60 minutes)

8. **Monitor response:** After inhibitor injection, subcooling margin should improve (decrease) within 15-30 minutes. If not, increase dose or consider additional measures.
9. **Check for plug formation:** If pressure has not recovered and flow continues to decline, escalate to plug remediation procedures (depressurization, never one-sided).
10. **Document the event:** Record the trigger, sensor values, action taken, and outcome for post-event analysis and threshold tuning.

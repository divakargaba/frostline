# Project Context

## Reference Reading

1. [Precision and recall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precision_and_recall)
   — Our eval scores catches vs false alarms; precision = how many alarms were real, recall = how many hydrate events we caught.

2. [Clathrate hydrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clathrate_hydrate)
   — Background on gas hydrates, the ice-like solids that form when gas molecules get trapped in frozen water cages under high pressure and low temperature. This is what plugs subsea lines and what our system warns about.
